# Enterprise IT & Operational Policies

## Incident Management Policy
- **Severity P1 (Critical):** Complete outage or major service degradation affecting over 25% of active users. SLA for initial response is 15 minutes. Must notify the engineering lead on call immediately.
- **Severity P2 (High):** Partial feature failure or performance degradation. SLA for initial response is 1 hour.
- **Severity P3 (Low):** Minor bug or cosmetic issue. Resolution scheduled for upcoming sprint.

## Password and Security Standards
- All employee accounts must enforce Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).
- Passwords must be a minimum of 16 characters and rotated every 90 days.
- Access to production databases requires prior written approval from the Security Ops team.

## Remote Work & Expense Reimbursement
- Home office equipment allowance is up to $500 per year per employee.
- Internet stipends of $50/month are processed automatically on the 1st of every month via payroll.
